### Introduction

Welcome to Dolphin Design Works (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our website: [https://www.dolphindesignworks.com](https://dolphindesignworks.com) (the "Site").

### 1\. Information We Collect

-   **Personal Information You Provide Directly:** Such as your name, email address, or phone number when you contact us or submit a form.
-   **Automatically-Collected Data:** Such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, collected via cookies and similar technologies.

### 2\. How We Use Your Information

-   To respond to inquiries and provide services.
-   To improve our website and user experience.
-   To monitor usage and analytics.

### 3\. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and related technologies to recognize returning visitors, improve site functionality, and analyze traffic. You may manage or disable cookies in your browser settings.

### 4\. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share information with:

-   **Service Providers** who support website operations (e.g., hosting, analytics).
-   **Legal Authorities** if required by law or to protect our rights.

### 5\. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for business purposes or to comply with legal obligations.

### 6\. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Users in the EU and California may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA.

### 7\. Children’s Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have, please contact us to request deletion.

### 8\. International Data Transfers

If you are outside the U.S., please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. We take steps to protect your data when this occurs.

### 9\. Security Measures

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data. However, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

### 10\. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a new “Last Updated” date. Please review it periodically.
